# Working in this repo

Before doing anything else, read `PROGRESS.md` and `features.json` in the
repo root. They record what a previous session did and did not finish. Run
`init.sh` to bring up the environment. Do not trust a feature's `status`
field in `features.json` without also running its `verify` command yourself
- the status reflects what the previous session believed at the time, not a
guarantee.
